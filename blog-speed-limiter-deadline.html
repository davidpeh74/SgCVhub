Lorry Speed Limiter Deadlines in Singapore: Is Your Lorry Affected?

Regulations · 4 min read · 16 Nov 2026

Singapore is rolling out speed limiter requirements for mid-weight lorries in stages. If you run lorries with a maximum laden weight (MLW) between 3,501kg and 12,000kg, one of the deadlines may apply to you soon, and for newer lorries the next one is 1 January 2027.

Which lorries need a speed limiter

The requirement covers lorries with an MLW from 3,501kg to 12,000kg, inclusive. The limiter must cap the lorry's speed at 60 km/h. Drivers must also stay within the road speed limit where it is lower. You can find the MLW on your vehicle documents.

Find your deadline

RegisteredMLWDeadline
Before 1 Jan 20185,001 to 12,000kg1 Jan 2026 (passed)
Before 1 Jan 20183,501 to 5,000kg1 Jul 2026 (passed)
1 Jan 2018 or later5,001 to 12,000kg1 Jan 2027
1 Jan 2018 or later3,501 to 5,000kg1 Jul 2027

Newly imported lorries in this weight range need a speed limiter from 1 January 2026 to be approved for use in Singapore.

How to comply

  • Use a Traffic Police Authorised Agent. Only an Authorised Agent appointed by the Traffic Police can supply, install, rectify or inspect speed limiters. Using an unauthorised agent is an offence. The list of agents is on the Singapore Police Force website.
  • Ask which type suits your lorry. ECU-based limiters are encouraged. If your vehicle cannot take one, a mechanical limiter is the alternative.
  • Display the label. The agent's label must be shown at the top right of the front windscreen, as seen from outside. Not displaying it is an offence.
  • Budget for it. Owners bear the cost of the limiter and its installation.
  • Do not leave it to the last week. Installing before the deadline is strongly encouraged, and missing the deadline is an offence.

What happens if you miss the deadline

The police said in July 2026 that failing to ensure a speed limiter is fitted carries a fine of up to $1,000, jail of up to three months, or both. They also said maximum fines are set to rise to $10,000 once the Land Transport and Related Matters Bill is passed, so check the current penalty before you rely on the lower figure.

Non-compliant lorries can be banned from being driven and cannot have their road tax renewed, and insurance coverage may be reduced. During a two-day operation on 1 and 2 July 2026, the Traffic Police checked companies with lorries covered by the 1 July deadline, and two lorries were towed away for not having speed limiters installed.

A driver caught speeding in a lorry that has a speed limiter can face both a speeding offence and a speed limiter offence.

A quick checklist

  1. Check each lorry's MLW and registration date.
  2. Match them to the table above to find the deadline.
  3. Book a Traffic Police Authorised Agent early.
  4. Display the agent's label on the windscreen.
  5. Keep the installation certificate with your vehicle records.
