FAQ

Common questions

Is leasing available for all three brands?
Yes — Foton, Mitsubishi Fuso, and Opel commercial vehicles can all be leased, subject to availability with Goldbell Leasing or our outside partners.
What's the minimum lease term?
Goldbell Leasing offers flexible short and long-term options. If you go through one of our outside leasing partners, a minimum 3-year term is preferred.
Who do I deal with — SgCVhub or the leasing company directly?
You start with us. We'll scope your requirements and connect you with the right leasing provider, so you're not navigating multiple parties on your own.

Ready to talk leasing?

Tell us your fleet size and timeline, and we'll put together a leasing option that fits.
