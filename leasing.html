SgCVhub — Leasing

Flexible leasing for every stage of your business

Not ready to commit to a purchase? We offer short and long-term leasing on Foton, Mitsubishi Fuso, and Opel commercial vehicles — with terms structured around whether you need a new or used vehicle.

Leasing Options

Two ways to lease, depending on your needs

The right term depends on whether you're leasing a new or used vehicle. Here's how each works.

New Vehicles
Long-Term Lease
Preferred term: 3 years

For businesses that want a new diesel or electric commercial vehicle without the upfront capital outlay of a purchase. A 3-year term is our preferred structure for new vehicles, giving you a predictable monthly cost over a period that matches typical fleet planning cycles.

  • Available on new diesel or EV models
  • Fixed monthly cost for easier budgeting
  • Suited to fleet operators and growing businesses
Used Vehicles
Short-Term Lease
Daily, weekly, or monthly

For businesses that need a vehicle for a defined period — a project, a seasonal peak, or covering a vehicle that's temporarily out of service. Mainly available on used diesel vehicles, with some used EV units also available depending on stock.

  • Mainly used diesel, with select used EV units
  • Flexible terms to match short-notice needs
  • No long-term commitment required

Leasing is available across our full range — Foton, Mitsubishi Fuso, and Opel — subject to current stock and vehicle condition.
