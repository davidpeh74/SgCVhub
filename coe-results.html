PQP for COE renewal

If you are renewing an existing vehicle's COE rather than buying a new vehicle, the Prevailing Quota Premium (PQP) applies. It is based on a rolling average of recent premiums, so it moves more gradually than a single bidding result.

Why Category C premiums are high

In Parliament in August 2026, the Transport Minister linked rising Category C premiums to more electric heavy goods vehicles and electric buses being registered since the Heavy Vehicle Zero Emissions Scheme began in January 2026. He said electric heavy vehicles rose from under 1% of registrations in 2025 to over 30% in June 2026. Category C is the only category with a quota growth provision, at 0.25% a year. The quota for November 2026 to January 2027 is 1,873, up 37 from the previous quarter.

What this means for you: COE is only one part of the cost. Premiums move every round, so the useful question is whether your business needs the vehicle now.
